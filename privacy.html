Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

The short version: the site stores only what it needs to keep your tasks in sync. Nothing is sold or shared, and there are no ads or trackers.

My To-Do List (to-do-blond-omega.vercel.app) is a small personal project run by Tanuj H. This page explains what the site stores about you, where it's kept, and why.

Using it as a guest

If you choose Continue as guest, your tasks are saved only in your browser's storage on that device. They're never sent anywhere. Clearing your browser's data for this site deletes them.

Signing in with Google

When you sign in, the site stores:

  • From your Google account: your name, your email address and a link to your profile picture. Google shares these when you choose to sign in. The site never sees your Google password.
  • Your tasks: each task's text, its date, whether it's done and when you finished it, and when it was created or last changed.
  • Sign-in records: Supabase, which runs the sign-in, keeps basic technical records of sign-ins (such as the time, IP address and browser) for security.

This is used only to sign you in and keep your tasks in sync across your devices. It isn't used for advertising or profiling, and it isn't sold or shared with anyone.

Who can see your tasks

Only you. The database's security rules let each account read and change only its own tasks. As the site's administrator, Tanuj H can technically access the database, but won't look at your tasks unless you ask for help or it's needed to fix a problem.

Services the site relies on

  • Supabase (supabase.com) stores your account and tasks, and runs sign-in.
  • Google provides “Sign in with Google” and the site's fonts (Google Fonts), so your browser contacts Google's servers when the page loads.
  • Vercel (vercel.com) hosts the website. Like most web hosts, it may log basic request details such as your IP address and browser.
  • jsDelivr (jsdelivr.com) delivers the code library the site uses to talk to Supabase.

Each of these handles data under its own privacy policy.

Your browser's storage

The site doesn't use cookies. It uses your browser's local storage to remember your theme (light or dark), which list you had open, your guest tasks, and your sign-in. While you're signed in, it also keeps a copy of your tasks so the site opens quickly and keeps working offline. Signing out removes that copy once all your changes have been saved.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept until you delete it. You can delete any task at any time. To delete your account and all of its tasks, email contact@example.com from the address you sign in with. It will be deleted within 30 days.

Children

The site isn't meant for children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email contact@example.com.
